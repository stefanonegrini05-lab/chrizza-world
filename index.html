CHAPTER 01 · OMETV & BEYOND

It all started with a white T-shirt.

You were in the dark on OmeTV. I could barely see you, so I illuminated my face with my white T-shirt — and for about two seconds, I saw yours. Then we started talking about movies and hobbies. I recommended The Hangover and flexed my bicep! 😂

You gave me your Instagram. I didn't text for two days… and you sent me the first Reel. When you told me you had watched the movie I recommended, I felt truly listened to. That little detail made me fall for you.

Months passed with long video calls, falling asleep together, dealing with distance, and choosing each other every single day. From my benzina ⛽ to my safe place, you changed my entire life for the better.
