CHAPTER 01 · OMETV

It all started with a white T-shirt.

You were in the dark on OmeTV. I could barely see you, so I illuminated my face with my white T-shirt — and for about two seconds, I saw yours. Then we started talking about movies and hobbies.

You asked if I liked reading books. I said I preferred movies and recommended The Hangover. Then I told you I went to the gym and flexed my bicep. Very smooth, Stefanino. 😂

You gave me your Instagram. I didn't text for two days… and you sent me the first Reel. When you told me you had actually watched the movie I recommended, I felt truly listened to.
