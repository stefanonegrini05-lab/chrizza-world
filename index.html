CHAPTERS OF US

Our story

It started in the most unexpected place. And somehow, it became my favourite story.

01

THE BEGINNING

It all started with a white T-shirt.

You were in the dark on OmeTV. I could barely see you, so I illuminated my face with my white T-shirt — and for about two seconds, I saw yours. Then we started talking about movies and hobbies.

You asked if I liked reading books. I said I preferred movies and TV shows, and recommended The Hangover. Then I told you I went to the gym and, of course, flexed my bicep. Very smooth, Stefanino. 😂

You gave me your Instagram. I didn't text for two days… and you sent me the first Reel. That little detail made me curious about you. We started talking about everything, and when you told me you had watched the movie I recommended, I felt listened to. I still remember that.
